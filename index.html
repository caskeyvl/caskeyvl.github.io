About

I’ve always loved building things and figuring out how they work. I’m a computer engineering student at CU Boulder in my last semester, graduating Fall 2026. My interests run wide — sometimes too wide — across control theory, embedded systems, AI, homelabbing, networking and network security, and computer architecture. I most enjoy working at the boundary between hardware and software; I think it’s the most interesting abstraction in the whole stack that makes a computer work.

In my spare time I ski and snowboard, climb, hike, play video games, and read.
